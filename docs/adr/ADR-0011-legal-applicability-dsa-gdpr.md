# ADR-0011: the legal applicability — DSA (EU) 2022/2065 Art. 26/27 and GDPR (EU) 2016/679 implemented to the code-expressible extent (Plan D-15 closure)

- **Status:** Accepted (owner-ordered execution — D-15 opened per the owner's standing order: «تعامل مع D-15 القانوني حسب الأصول القانونية والممارسات العالمية المجتمعية المعتمدة»)
- **Governing references (the official legal texts, EUR-Lex — the Publications Office Cellar renditions, retrieved verbatim):**
  - DSA — Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (CELEX 32022R2065), Article 26 *Advertising on online platforms*, Article 27 *Recommender system transparency*
  - GDPR — Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (CELEX 32016R0679), Articles 5, 15, 17, 20, 30, 32
  - Spring Boot reference — the framework mechanisms the implementation rides (Spring Data JPA specifications and JPQL queries; Spring's cache abstraction with the versioned namespace eviction discipline; Spring MVC REST controllers with OpenAPI annotations)
- **The honest boundary (the plan's own law, kept):** the plan states «لا استنتاج انطباق من قائمة تقنية» — no applicability CONCLUSION may be drawn from a technical checklist. This ADR does not declare the platform's legal status. It implements, line by line, every obligation of the cited articles that is CODE-EXPRESSIBLE, documents each against the official text, and leaves the qualified-legal-counsel sign-off (the operative-model and jurisdiction analysis) exactly where the plan puts it: an operational gate, not an engineering claim.

## The decision

1. **DSA Art. 26(1)(a) — real-time advertisement identification: the wire gains the `promoted` field.** The platform's advertisement is the paid promotion the ordering's first tier already speaks (`ProviderListingRepository`'s baked ORDER BY flag: a live per-impression/per-click campaign with remaining budget, or an admin featured window). The article's text: "Providers of online platforms that present advertisements on their online interfaces shall ensure that, for each specific advertisement presented to each individual recipient, the recipients of the service are able to identify, in a clear, concise and unambiguous manner and in real time" that the information is an advertisement. Implementation: `ListingSummary` (the row every public browse/search surface returns) gains the additive `boolean promoted` component, resolved per page in ONE batch query (`AdCampaignRepository.findLiveCampaignListingIds` — the SAME law the ORDER BY's EXISTS evaluates) plus the entity's own `promoted_until` against the injected Clock. The flag is baked WITH the order it describes (the versioned cache namespaces keep the pair consistent — `catalog-*-v4`, `search-results-v6`, the `ListingSummaryCacheContractFilesTest` bump law). The `ListingPromotionMarkingTest` pins the law: live campaign → true; future window → true; expired window without a live campaign → false; empty page → no query.
2. **DSA Art. 26(1)(b)/(c) — the advertiser and the payor: already on the wire, verified by construction.** The promoted listing's provider is displayed on every row (`providerName`); the paid campaign's payer IS the provider by the service's ownership construction (`AdCampaign.providerId` = the listing's owner — the V103 one-live-campaign law). No third-party payor path exists in the codebase; if one is ever built, this ADR's Art. 26(1)(a) marking is the surface it must extend.
3. **DSA Art. 26(2) — the commercial-communications declaration: the community surface gains it.** The article: providers "shall provide recipients of the service with a functionality to declare whether the content they provide is or contains commercial communications," and the declaration must be identifiable "in a clear and unambiguous manner and in real time." Implementation: `neighborhood_posts.declared_commercial` (V182, additive NOT NULL default false, Envers-audited), set ONLY by the publish factory from the author's own request (no moderation write exists), carried on EVERY read through `NeighborhoodPostView.declaredCommercial`. The declaration is the author's stored fact — the machine never flips it.
4. **DSA Art. 26(3) — no profiling-based advertising: true by construction, stated honestly.** The article prohibits presenting advertisements based on profiling using GDPR Art. 9 special categories. The codebase contains NO recipient-profile targeting: the campaign is per-listing (every recipient of the same query sees the same promotion), and no special-category data feeds any advertising decision. The negative is stated verbatim in the transparency surface's "What does NOT exist" group — the honest negative is part of the record, not a silence.
5. **DSA Art. 27(1)/(2) — the main parameters and their relative importance: the machine-truth surface.** The article requires the main parameters "in plain and intelligible language" plus "any options for the recipients of the service to modify or influence those main parameters." Implementation: the public `GET /api/v1/search/ranking-parameters` read (reference data in the categories-registry pattern — a CLOSED shape, no free HTML and no executable payloads, the ADR-0005 validator's own law) stating, per surface: the labeled promoted tier (its budget/window law and its first-within-sort placement), the Arabic full-text relevance (`ts_rank` over the 'arabic' configuration with the pg_trgm typo fallback), the whitelisted sorts, the V99 composite rating score, and the id ASC tiebreak — each with the reason for its relative importance (Art. 27(2)(b)). The `RankingParametersViewTest` pins the shape.
6. **DSA Art. 27(3) — the recipient's options: existing and enumerated.** The sort whitelist (price/newest/area/distance/rating), the radius triple, the filter axes, the labeled promoted tier itself, and the Art. 26(2) declaration are enumerated as the recipient's options on the same surface. The unsupported sort's 400 is the loud boundary — an option that silently ignored the recipient's choice would be the opposite of transparency.
7. **GDPR — the data-subject surfaces verified present, not rebuilt.** Art. 15/20 (access and portability): `UserDataExportService` aggregates bookings, reviews, messaging, media, notifications, saved searches, community, favorites and the identity core — one subject-scoped read. Art. 17 (erasure): the account self-deletion path exists (the journey-tested tombstone discipline). Art. 5/32 (principles and security): minimization is the field-level projection discipline every view already carries; the security measures are the repository's standing gates (CodeQL, Trivy, the dependency and container scans). The single documentation gap closed: **Art. 30 — the records of processing** (`docs/governance/records-of-processing.md`), stating purposes, categories, recipients, retention and security per processing activity, from the modules' actual schemas — nothing more, nothing invented.
8. **The official-texts method.** Every obligation above was implemented against the EUR-Lex article text retrieved for this ADR (the Publications Office Cellar renditions of CELEX 32022R2065 and 32016R0679), not against a summary or a secondary checklist. Where the plan's earlier citation described Art. 26 as "commercial communications differentiation," this ADR records the ENACTED text (the horizontal advertising article) — the official text is the governor, and the discrepancy is itself documentation.

## Consequences

- The recipient-facing wire now identifies every promoted row in real time, carries the author's commercial-content declarations, and exposes the ranking's main parameters and the recipient's options — the code-expressible obligations of DSA Art. 26/27 are closed end to end.
- The cache namespaces bumped (`catalog-*-v3 → v4`, `search-results-v5 → v6`) — the deploy itself evicts the stale shape; no runtime migration of cached values exists or is needed.
- The migrations gain V182 only; no existing migration was touched (the checksum guard's law; V182's line is registered in the manifest in this same change).
- The qualified legal decision — the operative model, the jurisdictions, the platform-size classification (the DSA's online-platform vs micro-enterprise analysis), and any counsel-owned counsel-language obligations — remains the owner's operational gate. What engineering claims here is exactly and only: the cited articles' technical obligations are implemented, tested, and documented against the official texts.
