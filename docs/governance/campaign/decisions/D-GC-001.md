# D-GC-001 — Gift-card bounded context, balance and financial liability

**Status:** PROPOSED — A0 synthesis for owner/A3 review; not approved for implementation.  
**Decision date:** 2026-10-31  
**Authority:** D-SALEOR-FULL-001, P3; campaign playbook §§2, 10 and 12.  
**Affected roots:** giftCard, giftCards, giftCardCurrencies, giftCardTags, giftCardSettings, all giftCard mutations and exportGiftCards; checkout/order/payment/refund integration. `exportGiftCards` has a separate sensitive-data disposition below.  
**Related decisions:** D-CHANNEL-001, D-TAX-001, D-SHOP-001, D-SETTINGS-001, ADR-0013.

## Context and measured gap

The canonical P3 matrix requires full gift-card lifecycle support and explicitly calls for a gift-card liability/balance owner, integration with pricing and ledger, concurrent double-redemption tests and ledger audit. The divergence registry currently records the gift-card family as OWNER_ADR_REQUIRED / PENDING-ADR (D-GC-001): no card entity, balance subledger or liability accounting owner exists. This ADR closes that ownership question only if accepted; it does not imply that any gift-card root is implemented.

A gift card is stored monetary value and an issuer liability. It is not a voucher, promotion or price reduction. A voucher/promotion changes the payable price; a gift card is a tender instrument whose remaining value must be accounted for. In Saleor 3.23.43, `GiftCard.boughtInChannel` is a nullable `String` carrying the purchase channel's slug, not a `Channel` object; it records provenance only and grants no redemption permission. The purchase channel is not the redemption-eligibility rule.

## Proposed decision

Create an independent **marketplace-gift-cards** bounded context for issuance, card identity, status, assignment, expiry policy, redemption eligibility, reservations and the card-level immutable movement history.

| Responsibility | Owner |
|---|---|
| Card lifecycle, issue/revoke/activate/deactivate, assignment, card code, available-balance projection, reservations and immutable gift-card movements | marketplace-gift-cards |
| Double-entry accounting, outstanding gift-card liability, issuer liability accounts, financial journal and reconciliation | marketplace-ledger |
| PSP execution and external payment attempts for non-gift-card tender | marketplace-payments |
| Checkout orchestration, tender allocation, order completion/cancellation, frozen tender allocation and historical order snapshot | `marketplace-orders` (`OrdersService implements CheckoutSpi`; contracts exposed through `shared/api`) |
| Promotion/voucher eligibility and price calculation (not gift-card stored value) | marketplace-pricing |
| Saleor GraphQL/Dashboard adapter | marketplace-app, as a thin adapter only |

The gift-card module owns card-specific movements and a rebuildable balance projection; the central ledger remains the accounting authority for financial liability and journal entries. No module may update another module's tables directly. The interface between gift cards and ledger must be explicit, transactionally safe and idempotent. Do not maintain two independently editable balances. `marketplace-orders` is the measured checkout/order orchestration owner: tender allocation joins the existing `place()` transaction through declared ports; it must not introduce a second checkout module or a distributed transaction. Seller credit and commission are derived from the order's frozen commercial settlement breakdown, never from one PSP intent amount. The ledger must post tender movements separately and reconcile their sum to the order total.

### Invariants

1. Every card has an issuer/liable legal party and an immutable currency. Issuer identity is explicit; issuance must not be enabled for all sellers until the merchant-of-record, legal issuer, settlement and seller-liability rules are settled. ADR-0013 currently records the platform as merchant of record; this card decision must be consistent with that policy and must not silently invent seller-issued cards.
2. `boughtInChannel` is an optional nullable `String` carrying the purchase-channel slug; it is provenance only, not a `Channel` object or authorization. Match the pinned Saleor baseline: a card is not assigned to one channel and may be redeemed in any channel whose currency matches the card currency. A currency mismatch is denied. Any narrower channel restriction is a deliberate project divergence and requires an owner-approved entry in the central divergence registry before it is implemented.
3. Balance changes are append-only movements: issue/credit, redemption/debit, adjustment, cancellation/release and refund/credit. Deletion or direct balance overwrite is prohibited. A persisted balance is a rebuildable projection, not an independent source of truth.
4. Checkout creates a time-bounded reservation; successful order/payment completion consumes it exactly once; failed/expired checkout releases it. Consume and release are durable operations with stable order-scoped idempotency keys (for example `gc-consume-<orderId>` and `gc-release-<orderId>`), not process-local locks or ephemeral flags. A scheduled recovery/reconciliation path must release expired reservations and safely replay interrupted operations. If a PSP success arrives after the reservation has expired or been released, the workflow must take a deterministic compensating path (re-reserve under the same constraints or refund/void the PSP leg); it must never silently consume a second customer's balance or mark a partially paid order complete. The contract must distinguish authorization/reservation from completed redemption.
5. Concurrent requests cannot spend the same value twice. Implement a PostgreSQL transactional invariant (row lock or conditional atomic update backed by a unique movement/idempotency key); prove it with real PostgreSQL/Testcontainers concurrency tests. In-memory locks are not an acceptable correctness boundary.
6. Partial redemption is supported only when the remaining balance and multi-tender allocation are explicit. At order placement, `marketplace-orders` persists an append-only frozen tender-allocation snapshot for the order (tender kind, amount in minor units, currency, movement/reservation reference and PSP intent reference where applicable). Payment, gift-card and ledger operations consume that same snapshot; they must never recompute the split from mutable settings. Seller credit and commission derive from the frozen commercial order breakdown (subtotal, discounts, tax and shipping under the approved settlement policy), not from a single PSP intent. Tender-level movements post separately and reconcile exactly: `Σ(tender amounts) = order total`. A mixed-tender order of 100 (gift card 60 + PSP 40) must produce the same seller credit and commission, to the minor unit, as the same basket paid wholly by PSP; otherwise gift-card value has been misclassified as a seller discount. Refunds create compensating movements linked to the original redemption and refund operation; they never rewrite prior movements. Partial refunds follow the frozen original tender allocation; where an exclusive-tax order is refunded, the gift-card/PSP allocation of the gross refund must preserve the frozen proportional net/tax treatment.
7. `GiftCardSettings { expiryType, expiryPeriod }` is a shop-global configuration/read surface in Saleor 3.23.43; it is not an automatic-expiry execution switch. Keep the settings representation separate from card expiry behavior. Expiry, dormant balances, forfeiture and breakage recognition remain disabled until jurisdiction-specific legal/accounting policy and the issuer's customer terms are approved. Until then, expiry settings must not cause cards to expire or accept an expiry operation that would have no effect; no automatic expiry/write-off may be inferred from a database timestamp.
8. Card codes are high-entropy bearer secrets. Generate with a cryptographically secure generator, store a one-way verifier where feasible, encrypt only where recoverable delivery is a real requirement, redact from logs/traces/analytics, and rate-limit lookup/redemption attempts. Assignment is not authorization by itself.
9. No gift-card root may return fake success, a fabricated balance, or a synthetic gift-card currency list.
10. Gift-card liability and gift-card tender movements are not provider earnings. They must not be credited to `ProviderBalance` or counted by `SettlementEligibilityService` as provider-settleable funds. Issuer-liability postings use dedicated ledger accounts and must leave provider settlement eligibility unchanged.

## Saleor surface and secret-handling boundaries

- `boughtInChannel` is exposed as nullable `String` (purchase-channel slug) and is read as provenance only; a slug never grants card access or changes currency eligibility.
- `giftCardSettings` exposes and updates Saleor's default-expiry configuration (`expiryType`, `expiryPeriod`) at shop scope. Saleor 3.23.43 gates the read/update surface with `MANAGE_GIFT_CARD`. The setting is not a background expiry scheduler and does not itself expire existing cards; under this proposal it must remain at `NEVER_EXPIRE` / behavior-disabled until the jurisdictional legal/accounting policy and issuer terms in invariant 7 are approved. The adapter must not accept `EXPIRY_PERIOD` as a successful no-op while expiry behavior is disabled.
- `exportGiftCards` is deprecated in the pinned 3.23.43 contract; Saleor's subsequent upstream API documentation marks gift-card export functionality as deprecated/removable and recommends querying the data and formatting it in the client. A7 must establish whether the pinned Dashboard 3.23.39 actually invokes this root. Do not build a new export job merely because the root exists. If the route is not used, keep it excluded. If the pinned Dashboard route must be retained, raw bearer `code` values are absent by default; any privileged raw-code export is a separate restricted capability requiring an approved permission at least as strong as `MANAGE_GIFT_CARD` (which protects `GiftCard.code`), actor/reason audit, bounded export scope, encrypted/short-lived delivery, log/trace redaction and negative tests against bulk disclosure. Until that capability is accepted, routes requiring raw codes remain disabled/unsupported.

## Alternatives considered

- **A — New marketplace-gift-cards owner with immutable subledger and central-ledger postings. RECOMMENDED.** Keeps stored-value lifecycle independent from discount policies and PSP mechanics while preserving a single central financial ledger.
- **B — Put the card balance in marketplace-payments. Rejected.** Payments owns external tender attempts and callbacks, not a durable customer stored-value product.
- **C — Model cards as vouchers/promotions in marketplace-pricing. Rejected.** It confuses liability-bearing tender with price reduction and cannot support balance/refund accounting faithfully.
- **D — Put gift cards directly in marketplace-ledger. Not selected.** The ledger should own financial accounting, not customer-facing card lifecycle, code delivery, assignment or redemption UX.

## Required acceptance evidence before clearing the divergence

- Modulith tests prove gift-card code cannot access pricing/payment/ledger persistence internals and no dependency cycle is introduced.
- PostgreSQL/Testcontainers tests prove two simultaneous redemptions cannot exceed balance; duplicate requests/webhooks are idempotent; reservation consume/release works under retries and expiry; durable operation records recover after process interruption and late PSP success after reservation expiry follows the compensating path.
- Mixed-tender parity tests prove a 100 order paid 60 by gift card + 40 by PSP credits the seller and calculates commission from the same frozen commercial settlement breakdown as the same basket paid wholly by PSP; tender movements reconcile to the order total to the minor unit.
- Tests prove gift-card liability postings do not change `ProviderBalance` or provider payout/settlement eligibility; issuer liability reconciliation equals outstanding unredeemed balances under the approved recognition policy.
- Dashboard tests prove the pinned route's use or non-use of deprecated `exportGiftCards`; an unused route is excluded, while any retained privileged export enforces at least the upstream `MANAGE_GIFT_CARD` protection used by `GiftCard.code`, bounded scope, actor/reason audit, encrypted/short-lived delivery and log/trace redaction. Raw codes are never included by default.
- Currency mismatch, issuer eligibility, inactive/revoked card, assignment, partial redemption, duplicate issue, adjustment authorization and refund allocation tests.
- Ledger reconciliation proves gift-card outstanding liability equals the aggregate unredeemed liability under the approved recognition policy, with immutable journal references.
- Secret-handling tests prove raw card codes do not enter logs, traces, audit payloads or exception messages.
- End-to-end journey proves channel → checkout → mixed tender → order → completed redemption → partial refund → compensating card movement and ledger read-back using the frozen tender-allocation snapshot; the refund preserves the original tender split and exclusive-tax gross/net proportions.
- Saleor 3.23.43 pinned source/tests establish any card/channel semantics before wire mapping. A similar-looking field is not sufficient evidence.

All gift-card lifecycle roots remain BLOCKED_BY_DECISION until the owner accepts this ADR and A0 updates the central records.

P3/P7 remain blocked for any retained Dashboard route that requires gift-card support. This ADR does not change SDL, migration allocation, root-inventory status or divergence status. A0 must update the divergence registry and exact root dispositions only after owner approval and after checking the pinned Dashboard's affected routes.
